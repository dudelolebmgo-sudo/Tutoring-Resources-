Everything I've built and used in my private tutoring: interactive practice sites, lesson slides, worksheets and study guides.

**Subjects:** English · Physics · Social Studies · Mathematics (coming soon)

**Students:** click any **Open** link. It works on a phone, tablet or laptop, with nothing to install.

## English

### Level D Word Lab (Units 1–3)

Learn the 20 words in each unit: meanings, listening, spelling, and writing your own sentences. Each unit ends with a quiz. Your progress is saved on your device.

- [Open the site](https://dudelolebmgo-sudo.github.io/Tutoring-Resources-/english/vocab-level-d-units-1-3/)

### FANBOYS & AWUBIS Workshop

Practice simple, compound and complex sentences: when to use FANBOYS or AWUBIS, where the comma goes, capitals and end marks. Includes a rule review and scored practice rounds.

- [Open the site](https://dudelolebmgo-sudo.github.io/Tutoring-Resources-/english/sentence-practice/)

## Physics

### Unit 1: Waves Study Hub

Vocabulary cards, the main ideas of each lesson, and a quiz. Filter by Lesson 1 (intro to waves), Lesson 2 (sound) or Lesson 3 (light).

- [Open the site](https://dudelolebmgo-sudo.github.io/Tutoring-Resources-/physics/waves-unit-1/)

### Unit 1: Waves slides

Each lesson ends with a short "Check yourself" quiz and an answer slide.

- **Lesson 1: Introduction to Waves.** Energy vs. matter, mechanical and EM waves, transverse and longitudinal waves, amplitude, wavelength, frequency, and wave speed.
  [Slides (PDF)](physics/presentations/unit-1-lesson-1-intro-to-waves.pdf)
- **Lesson 2: The Behavior of Mechanical Waves.** Sound, loudness and pitch, decibels, absorption, transmission and reflection, the Doppler effect, sonar and echolocation.
  [Slides (PDF)](physics/presentations/unit-1-lesson-2-mechanical-waves.pdf)
- **Lesson 3: Light Waves.** EM waves, the electromagnetic spectrum, the speed of light, energy and frequency, and why objects have color.
  [Slides (PDF)](physics/presentations/unit-1-lesson-3-light-waves.pdf)

### Electricity & Magnetism: Unit Test Practice

A 20-question worksheet: magnets, charge, fields, electromagnets and generators (multiple choice); Ohm's law and circuits (calculations); induction, wind turbines and solar energy (short answer).

- [View (PDF)](physics/worksheets/electricity-magnetism-practice.pdf)
- [Download (Word, editable)](physics/worksheets/electricity-magnetism-practice.docx)

## How this repo is organized

Everything is grouped by subject:

- `english/`
  - `vocab-level-d-units-1-3/`: Word Lab site
  - `sentence-practice/`: FANBOYS & AWUBIS site
- `physics/`
  - `waves-unit-1/`: Waves Study Hub site
  - `presentations/`: lesson slides (PDF)
  - `worksheets/`: practice sheets (PDF and Word)
- `social-studies/`
  - `maya-study-guide/`: study guide (web page and PDF)

Each site is a single `index.html` file. To use one offline, download it and open it in any browser.
